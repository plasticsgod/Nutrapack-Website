Counterfeits Are Coming for Your Products: How Security Features Help

When a product gets popular, copies follow. Custom security features make the real one easy to recognize and the fake hard to make.

Authentic

The moment a product sells out or a bottle becomes a collector's item, copies start showing up online. Counterfeits take sales, and worse, they put your name on products you didn't make.

Why printed labels alone aren't enough

A standard printed label can be scanned, reprinted and stuck on a fake in an afternoon. To protect a product, you need features that a scanner or copier can't reproduce.

What security features add

  • Custom holographic effects: color-shifting designs built around your logo. They look premium on the real product and don't survive a scan or photocopy.
  • Micro text: lettering so fine that a copy turns it into a blur.
  • Anti-copy patterns: fine-line backgrounds that break apart, or reveal hidden marks, when someone tries to copy them.

Where it makes the biggest difference

  • Supplements: high-value tubs and bottles sold online, where knockoffs are common.
  • Limited editions: drops and collabs where the label is part of the collectible.
  • Spirits: bottles and caps where authenticity matters to buyers.
  • And more: beverages, cosmetics and any product worth protecting.

It's also brand enhancement

A well-designed security label makes the genuine product look and feel more premium. Over time customers learn what the real one looks like, which makes fakes easier for them to spot.
