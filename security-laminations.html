FAQ

Security lamination questions

What is a security lamination?

A custom laminate applied to a product or its label that carries anti-counterfeit features: holographic effects, micro text and anti-copy patterns designed around your brand.

Can a counterfeiter copy it?

The features are chosen because a scanner or copier can't reproduce them. A copy loses the holographic color shift, the micro text blurs, and anti-copy patterns break apart, so a fake is easier to spot.

What products can you protect?

Hats, spirits bottles, jerseys and more, including merch, apparel and packaged products like supplements.

Is each lamination custom?

Yes. Each lamination is designed around your brand, so it also works as brand enhancement on the shelf.
