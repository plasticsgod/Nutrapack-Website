About NutraPack

The partner big suppliers won't call back

NutraPack is a packaging supplier and printing partner for supplement, beverage, spirits and cosmetic brands worldwide.

Many large packaging suppliers ask for 50,000 units or more before they will quote. We work with emerging brands, take your artwork through press matching, label printing and quality control, and consolidate tubs, lids, labels, stickers and sleeves under one purchase order and one timeline.

Founders kept telling us the same story: every supplier conversation ended at "we don't take orders under 50,000 units," "design isn't included," or "labels are a different vendor." So we built the partner we wished existed.

Now we also protect what you make, with custom security features that a scanner or copier can't reproduce.

FAQ

Questions brands ask first

What does NutraPack do?

NutraPack is a packaging, printing and brand protection partner. We source custom packaging like supplement tubs, pouches, paper tubes and stick packs, print labels, stickers and shrink sleeves, help with packaging and label design, and make anti-counterfeit security features, all under one purchase order.

Do you print supplement labels and stickers?

Yes. We print custom supplement labels, product labels and stickers, including die-cut and roll labels, press-matched to your Pantone colors and quality-checked before they ship. See label and sticker printing.

What is your minimum order?

Many large suppliers ask for 50,000 units or more. Tell us your quantity in a brief and we will tell you what works for your launch.

Who answers my quote request?

A real person, not a bot. Your account lead replies with materials, MOQ tiers, lead times and unit cost.

Do you ship to my co-manufacturer?

Yes. We ship finished components wherever your product gets filled: your co-manufacturer, co-packer, fulfillment partner or warehouse.

What are security features?

Custom security labels for supplements, spirits, beverages and more, with holographic effects, micro text and anti-copy patterns that a scanner or copier can't reproduce. They make the real product look more premium and make fakes easier to spot. See security features.
