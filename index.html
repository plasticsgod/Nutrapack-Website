About NutraPack

The partner big suppliers won't call back

NutraPack is a packaging supplier and printing partner based in Huntsville, Texas, working with supplement, beverage, spirits, apparel and cosmetic brands across the US.

Big packaging suppliers want 50,000 units before they pick up the phone. We start at 10,000, take your artwork through press matching, label printing and quality control, and consolidate tubs, lids, labels, stickers and sleeves under one purchase order and one timeline.

Now we also protect what you make: custom security laminations that a scanner or copier can't reproduce. More about us.

FAQ

Questions brands ask first

What does NutraPack do?

NutraPack is a packaging, printing and brand protection partner based in Texas. We source custom packaging like supplement tubs, pouches, paper tubes and stick packs, print labels, stickers and shrink sleeves, help with packaging and label design, and make anti-counterfeit security laminations, all under one purchase order.

Do you print supplement labels and stickers?

Yes. We print custom supplement labels, product labels and stickers, including die-cut and roll labels, press-matched to your Pantone colors and quality-checked before they ship. See label and sticker printing.

What is your minimum order?

Most packaging and printing runs start at 10,000 units, well below the 50,000 many large suppliers ask for. Send a brief and we will tell you what works for your quantity.

How fast do I get a quote?

Within 48 hours. A real person replies with materials, MOQ tiers, lead times and unit cost.

Do you ship to my co-manufacturer?

Yes. We ship finished components wherever your product gets filled: your co-manufacturer, co-packer, fulfillment partner or warehouse.

What are security laminations?

Custom laminations for hats, spirits, jerseys and more, with holographic effects, micro text and anti-copy patterns that a scanner or copier can't reproduce. They make the real product look more premium and make fakes easier to spot. See security laminations.
