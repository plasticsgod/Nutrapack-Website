Privacy policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

This policy explains what information NutraPack LLC ("NutraPack", "we") collects through nutrapack.co and how we use it. Questions: contact@nutrapack.co.

What we collect

  • What you send us. When you fill in a brief or quote form, we receive what you enter, such as your name, email, brand, what you need, quantities, sizes, colors, shipping location and any message.
  • Emails. If you email us, we receive your email address and message.
  • Basic technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log standard technical information (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) to keep the site running and secure.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed, how visitors arrived, the type of device and approximate location. Google Analytics uses cookies for this. We do not use it for advertising, and Google signals and ad personalization are turned off. For visitors in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, analytics cookies are off by default. You can also block cookies in your browser settings or use Google's opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

How we use it

  • To reply to your brief, prepare quotes and work with you on your order.
  • To keep the site secure and working, and to understand which pages are useful so we can improve them.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who handles it for us

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox by Formspree. Site analytics are provided by Google Analytics. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and the 3D viewer library from cdnjs (Cloudflare). These providers process data only to provide their service.

How long we keep it

We keep briefs and emails as long as needed to respond and to keep records of our work with you, then delete them when they are no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you sent us by emailing contact@nutrapack.co. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights, and we will honor them.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed to children under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
