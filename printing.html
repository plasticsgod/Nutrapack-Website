FAQ

Label & sticker printing questions

Do you print custom supplement labels?

Yes. We print supplement labels for tubs, bottles, jars and pouches: front, back and neck labels, press-matched to your colors and quality-checked before they ship.

Are you a sticker printer too?

Yes. Stickers and labels are printed the same way, so we print custom stickers, die-cut stickers and roll labels for products, packaging and promotions.

Can you print shrink sleeves for cans?

Yes, including full-body shrink sleeves for 12 oz cans and bottles, a common choice for energy drinks and beverages.

Do you design labels and packaging?

We help with label and packaging design: turning your idea into a real spec, preparing print-ready artwork and showing you how it looks before anything is printed. Already have a designer? Send their files and we take it from there.

What files do you need?

Bring your own artwork or send us your specs. We work with what you have and check it for print before production.

How long does printing take?

It depends on the format and quantity. Your quote includes the full lead time for your job.
