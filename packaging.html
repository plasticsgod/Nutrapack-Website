FAQ

Custom packaging questions

What packaging solutions do you offer?

Custom HDPE supplement tubs in five sizes (8 to 55 oz) with matching lids and induction liners, stand-up and flat-bottom pouches, paper tubes, stick packs from 2 g to 30 g, and cosmetic packaging like airless bottles, droppers and jars. We also print the labels and shrink sleeves and add security features, so the whole package ships to your co-manufacturer under one PO.

What tub sizes do you offer?

Five sizes that share one design: 8 oz (60 mm), 10 oz (60 mm), 20 oz (89 mm), 25 oz (89 mm) and 55 oz (120 mm). They hold from about 120 capsules up to about 750 g of powder.

Can you match my brand color?

Yes. Tubs and lids are made to order in any coated Pantone color, with a matte or gloss spray coating.

What is the minimum order for custom supplement packaging?

Tell us your quantity in the brief and we will quote what works for your launch.

Can I see my tub before ordering?

Yes. Our 3D tub designer lets you pick a size and color and see your tub in 3D before we make a single one.

Do you also print the labels?

Yes. We print supplement labels and shrink sleeves to fit the packaging, so tubs, lids and labels ship together under one PO. See label printing.
