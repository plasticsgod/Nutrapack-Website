How it works Sketch to shelf.

One partner across the whole package. Five steps, no hand-offs: a clear path from your first idea to finished packaging, labels and printed components on a pallet.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Send us a sketch, a competitor you admire, or a Pinterest board. We translate whatever you have into a real packaging spec: materials, dimensions, finishes and decoration method.

    If you don't know what you want yet, that's fine. Most founders don't. We ask the right questions to get there.

    Anything
    You get back
    A spec sheet
    Time
    Same week

    Print

    Your designer hands you the file and we take it from there, or we help with label and packaging design. Press matching, label printing, decoration and quality control, coordinated across our supplier network so you get finished components, not "almost there" samples.

    You provide
    Artwork or specs
    We provide
    Printed components
    Time
    About 14 days

    Source

    This is where most boutique brands lose weeks. We consolidate every component, from tubs and caps to labels, sleeves and secondaries, under one timeline and one purchase order. No three-vendor scramble.

    Produce

    Press-pass approval before the run goes wide. Line QA throughout production. A sealed sample box mailed to your office before anything ships, so the first time you see your finished product isn't on a pallet.
